About Automind Systems

Built to End Manual Logistics Bloat

We exist because freight forwarders and shipping companies are drowning in spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected systems. Automind Systems combines developer-grade custom code with cutting-edge AI automation to eliminate the manual work holding your operations back. We're also the makers of AuditLog, our own product for auditing logistics invoices and supplier statements.

Hybrid Technology Approach: Custom Code Power + AI Speed

We don't choose between no-code tools and custom development—we architect the right mix of Python, Node.js, Webhooks, REST APIs with OpenAI, Claude, Make.com & Zapier for every client.

Why We Started

Logistics teams lose real hours every week to repetitive tasks—copying invoice data, checking carrier portals, replying to status emails. Off-the-shelf software rarely fits unique workflows, and pure no-code tools hit limits fast.

Automind Systems was founded to bridge that gap: write the custom integrations your TMS and ERP need, then layer AI on top to read documents, classify emails, and trigger workflows automatically. AuditLog, our own product, grew directly out of this work — built and tested against real multi-year freight statements.

The result? Operations that run with developer reliability and AI speed—without hiring a full in-house engineering team.
